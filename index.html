Jab We Met

At Wells Fargo's hall, where coding was king,
They met for a meeting—little did they know what fate would bring!
A planned chat it was, but the universe had its scheme,
From casual talks grew a mischievous little dream.

With nervous, late whispers, their romance took hold,
Through secret messages, a story to be told.
The first date arrived, a promise they couldn't ignore,
Her voice, like a favorite song they hadn't heard before.

First look, a bright grin, a heart's happy drum,
Burritos and jokes, proving this was "the one."
A bold declaration, no more hiding the truth,
Through silly debates and tough times, they found proof.

With a cheerleading squad of friends standing by,
Their hilarious journey began, beneath a lucky sky.
Three years have flown by! Their bright love's no trick,
Their bond's extra strong, like cement and a brick.

Adventures and chaos, a future so bright,
Two goofy souls tethered, laughing with all of their might.
A magical start, right from that very first hello,
The rest, as they say, is a wonderful show!
